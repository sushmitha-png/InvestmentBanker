The enterprise SaaS model with strong ARR is inherently resilient. However, it is not immune to stress. The model would break under the following conditions:
*   **Elevated Churn:** A significant increase in customer churn in its core markets or in new expansion territories would immediately impact ARR growth and valuation.
*   **Technological Disruption:** A new AI technology or competitor could render its solution obsolete, though this is a risk for the entire sector.
*   **Macroeconomic Downturn:** A severe recession in North America and Europe could lead to elongated sales cycles and reduced enterprise IT budgets, slowing new customer acquisition.

### 8. Execution & Expansion Risk

The plan to expand into North America and Europe is the primary use of our funds and also a primary risk. Competing in these mature markets requires significant investment in local sales and marketing talent, product localization, and navigating a different competitive landscape. Failure to gain traction would strand our invested capital and severely impair the growth story required for a successful IPO.

The primary fragility is the unexplained valuation gap. If this discount is due to a hidden problem (e.g., customer concentration, litigation, tech obsolescence), our entire thesis is void. Assuming the business is clean, the key downside scenarios are:
