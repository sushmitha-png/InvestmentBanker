### **PRE-IPO INVESTMENT COMMITTEE MEMORANDUM**

**DATE:** [Current Date]
**SUBJECT:** Project AI SaaS - Proposed Primary Investment of ₹350.0 Cr

### 1. Executive Verdict & Conviction

**Verdict: CAUTIOUS BUY**

The "Buy" recommendation is driven by a compelling valuation entry point. The deal-implied post-money entry multiple of **7.6x** forward EBITDA represents a significant **52.3% discount** to our base-case fair value EV of ₹6374.4 Cr. This dislocation, combined with the company's exceptional financial profile—including a 36.8% Revenue CAGR and a 56.9% EBITDA CAGR—presents a clear path to a strong return, with a base-case MOIC of **5.42x**.

The "Cautious" qualifier is critical. The valuation discrepancy flagged in our analysis is substantial and requires explanation. Furthermore, key information gaps regarding management quality and balance sheet leverage (Net Debt) introduce material unquantified risks. The planned expansion into competitive Western markets is capital-intensive and carries significant execution risk. Our conviction is therefore tempered pending satisfactory diligence on these key points.

### 2. Investment Thesis – What MUST Go Right

For this investment to succeed, the following conditions must be met:
1.  **Sustained Growth & Margin Expansion:** The company must continue its high-growth trajectory (in line with the 30.0% EBITDA CAGR assumed for the IPO) and maintain or improve its 17.6% EBITDA margin through operating leverage.
2.  **Successful Geographic Expansion:** The ₹350.0 Cr primary capital must be deployed effectively to establish a meaningful footprint in North America and Europe, converting market opportunity into tangible revenue.
3.  **Multiple Re-rating at Exit:** The market must value the company at a public multiple consistent with our exit assumptions (base case of **24.5x** EV/EBITDA), allowing us to realize the valuation uplift from our discounted entry point.
4.  **Valuation Gap Justification:** The 52.3% discount must be attributable to factors we can accept (e.g., founder dilution sensitivity, need for a strategic partner) and not to undisclosed negative information.

### 3. Financial Quality & Sustainability Analysis

The company exhibits a high-quality financial profile characteristic of a top-tier SaaS business. Revenue growth is robust, with a forward projection of ₹19090.0 mn from a base of ₹13695.0 mn. The subscription model provides revenue predictability, and low customer churn indicates a sticky product. The forward EBITDA margin of 17.6% is healthy and expected to expand, as evidenced by historical growth patterns. However, the absence of Net Debt data is a notable omission, preventing a complete assessment of financial health and leverage.

### 4. Growth Quality & Operating Leverage Assessment

The company demonstrates strong operating leverage, a key indicator of a scalable and efficient business model. The historical EBITDA CAGR of **56.9%** significantly outpaces the Revenue CAGR of **36.8%**. This confirms that as the company scales, its profitability is growing at an accelerated rate, a highly desirable trait for a pre-IPO asset. This trend is fundamental to our projection of ₹6733.0 mn in EBITDA at IPO.

### 5. Valuation & Multiple Justification

The investment's primary appeal lies in its valuation.
*   **Entry Multiple:** We are entering at a deal-implied **post-money multiple of 7.6x** forward EBITDA (pre-money: **6.8x**). This is substantially below the listed comps' trading median of 28.0x and the transaction median of 21.0x.
*   **Discount to Fair Value:** The deal is priced at a **52.3% discount** to our internally calculated base-case fair value EV of ₹6374.4 Cr.
*   **Exit Assumptions:** Our base-case exit scenario assumes an IPO in 2 years at an EV/EBITDA multiple of **24.5x**, resulting in a projected IPO EV of **₹164958.5 mn**. This implies a P/E ratio of **38.1x** at IPO, which is reasonable for a high-growth SaaS company in a favorable market.
*   **Projected Returns:** These assumptions translate directly into a compelling return profile, with a base-case **Upside vs entry of 442.0%** and a **MOIC of 5.42x**. The full range of potential MOIC is **4.65x to 6.19x**.

### 6. Capital Structure & Balance Sheet Risk

The capital structure is a key area for diligence. With Net Debt figures unavailable, we cannot assess leverage risk or the company's current solvency. The proposed transaction is a primary infusion of ₹350.0 Cr, which will de-risk the balance sheet and provide a war chest for the planned expansion. We must confirm the existing debt and off-balance-sheet liabilities before proceeding.

### 7. Business Model Robustness (What Breaks Under Stress)

The subscription-based SaaS model is inherently resilient. However, it is not immune to stress. The model could break under the following conditions:
*   **Technological Disruption:** A new AI technology or competitor renders the company's product obsolete.
*   **Intensified Competition:** In a fragmented market, a price war could erode the 17.6% EBITDA margin.
*   **Macroeconomic Downturn:** A recession could slow enterprise IT spending, impacting new customer acquisition and ARR growth, challenging the 40%+ growth rate.

### 8. Execution & Expansion Risk

The plan to expand into North America and Europe is ambitious and represents the most significant operational risk. Success requires navigating different regulatory environments, building local sales and support teams, and competing against established incumbents. Failure to execute this expansion would invalidate a core pillar of the growth story underpinning our exit valuation.

### 9. Governance & IPO Readiness

The lack of information on management quality is a major concern. For a pre-IPO investment, we are backing the team as much as the business. A 2-year timeline to IPO is aggressive and requires immediate focus on building a public-company-ready board, implementing robust financial controls (IFRS/GAAP), and enhancing corporate governance. The company is currently rated as "Needs Work" for IPO readiness.

### 10. Downside Scenarios & Fragility Analysis

A key fragility is the dependency on favorable public market conditions for a successful IPO. A market downturn could compress exit multiples below our low-end assumption of **21.0x**, severely impacting returns. If the North American expansion fails to gain traction, the projected EBITDA at IPO of **₹6733.0 mn** may not be achievable, leading to a lower exit valuation even if multiples hold steady.

### 11. Strategic Optionality (IPO vs M&A vs Delay)

While the primary exit path is an IPO, the market's fragmented and consolidating nature provides a strong secondary option. The company could become an attractive acquisition target for larger global SaaS players seeking to enter the AI space or expand their product suite. This provides a valuable alternative liquidity path if IPO market conditions are unfavorable, adding a layer of downside protection.

### 12. Key Red Flags

1.  **Capital Consistency Inconsistency:** The analysis explicitly flags that the deal valuation is inconsistent with our own. The deal-implied EV of ₹30434.8 mn is **52.3%** lower than our fair value EV of ₹63744 mn. We must understand the reason for this massive gap. Is the seller distressed? Are we missing a key risk? Is our valuation model flawed?
2.  **Unknown Management Quality:** We have no data on the founders or the senior leadership team. Investing pre-IPO without confidence in management is unacceptable. This is a critical diligence workstream.
3.  **Incomplete Financials:** The absence of Net Debt data is a material omission that obscures the true risk profile of the company's balance sheet.

*   **Base Case (2-Year Horizon):** The company effectively utilizes the ₹350.0 Cr investment to execute its expansion plan. It achieves the projected EBITDA of ₹6733.0 mn and successfully lists in 2 years at an exit multiple of **24.5x**, delivering the projected **5.42x MOIC**.
