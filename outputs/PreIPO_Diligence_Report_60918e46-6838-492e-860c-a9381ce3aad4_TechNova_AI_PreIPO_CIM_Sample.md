### 7. Business Model Robustness (What Breaks Under Stress)

The subscription-based SaaS model for enterprise AI is inherently resilient. However, it is not immune to stress.

*   **Stress Test 1: Technological Disruption:** If a new, more efficient AI model (e.g., a foundational model from a hyperscaler) can replicate the company's value proposition at a lower cost, its "solution" could become a commoditized "feature." *   **Stress Test 2: Economic Downturn:** While enterprise software is sticky, a severe recession could lead to longer sales cycles, down-selling from existing clients, and increased pricing pressure, impacting both ARR growth and margins.
*   **Stress Test 3: Data Privacy & Regulation:** As an AI company operating in Europe and North America, the business is exposed to evolving regulations like GDPR and AI-specific legislation. A compliance failure could result in significant fines and reputational damage.
